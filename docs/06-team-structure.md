# Team Structure

Organizer roles for the Robotics Pizza Hackathon. See [Launch Plan](05-launch-plan.md) for the
timeline these roles operate against.

## 1. Event Director / Product Lead

**Before event — owns:** concept; scope; budget; timeline; cross-team decisions; success criteria.

**During event:** overall command; escalation decisions; schedule control.

Should not be primary robot troubleshooter.

## 2. Technical Lead

**Before event — owns:** benchmark architecture; software freeze; challenge definitions; reference
implementation; go/no-go technical decisions.

**During event:** highest technical escalation; competition integrity; model/evaluation disputes.

## 3. Robot Hardware Lead

**Owns:** SO-101 assembly; calibration; spare parts; station setup; physical resets; robot failure
recovery.

**Event staffing:** recommended 1 hardware technician per approximately 4 team stations during peak
hours.

## 4. ML / Data Lead

**Owns:** LeRobot datasets; training templates; SmolVLA/ACT baselines; checkpoint format; data
validation; dataset publishing.

**During event:** training troubleshooting; dataset corruption diagnosis; model submission support.

## 5. Simulation Lead

**Owns:** LeIsaac environment; pizza task assets; simulation onboarding; randomization; sim
benchmark consistency.

This can be a part-time role after simulator V1 is stable.

## 6. Compute / Infrastructure Lead

**Owns:** GPU accounts; quotas; network; authentication; shared storage; model submission;
leaderboard backend; backups.

Must prepare a local fallback for critical files.

## 7. Competition Operations Lead

**Owns:** evaluation queue; standardized resets; judges; scoring; leaderboard; model freeze;
challenge seeds.

During finals this person effectively runs race control.

## 8. Participant Experience Lead

**Owns:** registration; team formation; onboarding; handbook; help desk; mentor coordination; event
communications.

## 9. Sponsorship / Partnerships Lead

**Owns:** sponsor pipeline; commercial packages; contracts; logo approvals; sponsor deliverables;
partner speakers; prize commitments.

## 10. Marketing & Community Lead

**Owns:** campaign; social accounts; community partnerships; participant acquisition; newsletters;
announcements; post-event distribution.

## 11. Media Lead

**Owns:** visual identity; photography; video; livestream; editing; interview capture; sponsor
content.

Marketing and Media can be combined pre-event but should be separate during finals.

## 12. Venue / Safety / Logistics Lead

**Owns:** venue; food; electrical distribution; tables; security; access; first aid; insurance;
safety brief.

## Minimum Core Team

For a small inaugural event:

| Person | Combined responsibility |
|---|---|
| 1 | Event/Product + Sponsorship |
| 2 | Technical + ML/Data |
| 3 | Hardware |
| 4 | Infra + Simulation |
| 5 | Participant Ops + Logistics |
| 6 | Marketing + Media |

Add volunteers and mentors for event weekend.
