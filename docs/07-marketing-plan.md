# Marketing Plan

## Target Audience

**Primary:** ML engineers; robotics engineers; AI researchers; graduate students; strong
undergraduate builders; AI startup engineers.

**Secondary:** founders; computer-vision engineers; embedded engineers; makers.

## Acquisition Funnel

For an 8–12 team event, target approximately:

- 2,000–4,000 qualified landing-page visitors
- 150–250 applicants
- 50–80 shortlisted participants
- 30–45 final participants
- 8–12 teams

Maintain a waitlist because robotics events experience cancellation and team changes.

## Campaign Pillars

1. **Spectacle** — robot makes pizza. This drives clicks.
2. **Technical depth** — fine-tune a VLA on real-world robot data. This attracts serious
   participants.
3. **Accessibility** — you don't need to own a robot. This expands the funnel.
4. **Competition** — your policy runs against other teams on identical hardware. This creates
   narrative.
5. **Generalization** — the final configuration is hidden until evaluation. This creates
   legitimacy.

## Content Calendar

**T-6 weeks — Announcement.** Hero video. Message: *"We're giving teams robots and 48 hours to
teach them to make pizza."*

**T-5 weeks — Technical post.** Show: Teleoperation → Dataset → VLA → Robot. Call for applicants.

**T-4 weeks — Challenge reveal.** Release Precision Topping. Show physical robot attempting it.
Failures are valuable content.

**T-3 weeks — Model content.** Show: 50 demonstrations; training; first autonomous attempt.
Headline: *"How many demonstrations does a robot need to learn pepperoni?"*

**T-2 weeks — Teams / sponsors.** Announce: selected participants; mentors; sponsors; prize pool.

**T-1 week — Mystery Pizza teaser.** Explain that the final configuration will be hidden. Do not
reveal it.

## Event Content

Publish continuously: teleoperation clips; first failures; first successes; training dashboards;
team interviews; leaderboard updates; funny robot mistakes; mentor commentary.

Avoid presenting every failure as a disaster — failure is the narrative of robot learning.

## Post-Event Content

1. 45–90 second hero recap
2. winning run
3. failure compilation
4. technical retrospective
5. dataset announcement
6. winning team interview
7. sponsor impact post
8. "What we learned from X robot episodes" article
